## 🙋 About Me

I'm Louis, a hobby developer from Germany. I work on a bunch of different projects at the same time but I'm mostly known for the Random Item Giver Datapack. You should also check out my other projects though, they are great as well! Because of university, I'm not as active as I used to be but I'm still trying to get out some updates every now and then.

## 🔧 Projects that I'm working on
My main projects are currently the [Random Item Giver Datapack](https://github.com/Seeloewen/Random-Item-Giver-Datapack), the [Random Item Giver Updater](https://github.com/Seeloewen/Random-Item-Giver-Updater) and [SeeloewenCraft](https://github.com/Seeloewen/SeeloewenCraft).

My most important side projects are [SeeloewenMapper](https://github.com/Seeloewen/SeeloewenMapper), [Seeloewen Sync](https://github.com/Seeloewen/Seeloewen-Sync) and [Seeloewen Shutdown](https://github.com/Seeloewen/Seeloewen-Shutdown) including some smaller projects that I maintain from time to time.
